Getting Started

Smooth

Super smooth positioning without scroll lag or jank. Works whether the sidebar is shorter or taller than the viewport.

Multiple sidebars

Stick any number of sidebars at once — 3 columns, 4 columns, or your whole page layout. Scroll the demos above.

Responsive

Automatically disables sticky behavior when the layout stacks, with minWidth and disableOnResponsiveLayouts.

Getting Started

Your sidebar should live inside the container that defines its scrolling boundary. Then pass an element or selector — or an array for multiple sidebars.

<div id="main-content">
  <aside id="sidebar">
    <!-- Sidebar content -->
  </aside>
  <div id="content">
    <!-- Main content -->
  </div>
</div>
import StickyGum from "stickygum";

new StickyGum({
  container: "#main-content",
  sidebar: "#sidebar",
  offsetTop: 30,
  offsetBottom: 30
});

Once the container reaches its bottom edge, StickyGum stops the sidebar there instead of letting it scroll beyond the container. Call unbind() to remove all listeners and restore the sidebar.

Options

OptionTypeDefaultDescription
sidebarstring | Element | Element[]—Sidebar element(s) to make sticky.
containerstring | ElementSidebar parentContainer that limits the sticky area.
offsetTopnumber0Space between the top of the viewport and the sidebar.
offsetBottomnumber0Space between the bottom of the viewport and the sidebar.
updateSidebarHeightbooleantrueUpdates sidebar height dynamically while scrolling.
minWidthnumber0Disables StickyGum below this viewport width.
disableOnResponsiveLayoutsbooleantrueDisables sticky behavior when the layout becomes stacked.
sidebarBehavior"modern" | "stick-to-top" | "stick-to-bottom""modern"Controls how the sidebar behaves while scrolling.
defaultPositionstring"relative"Initial CSS position before StickyGum starts.
verbosebooleanfalseLogs helpful warnings during initialization.
